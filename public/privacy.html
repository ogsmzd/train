Privacy policy

Train · last updated 25 October 2026

Train is a personal training log built for, and used by, one person: its owner. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts other than the owner's.

What data the app handles

  • Training data you enter: workouts, sets, weights, mat sessions, morning check-ins, notes and feel ratings.
  • If WHOOP is connected: recovery, sleep, cycle (strain) and workout data, read through the official WHOOP API with your permission.

Where it is stored

Training data is stored on your own device, in the browser's local storage. If cloud sync is enabled, it is also stored in a private database that only the owner can access. WHOOP access tokens are kept server-side and are never exposed in the app.

How it is used

Data is used only to show your training history, calculate suggested weights and readiness, and produce summaries for your own review. It is not sold, shared with third parties, used for advertising, or used to train any model.

Your control

You can export or delete all app data at any time from the app's More screen. You can disconnect WHOOP at any time from the app or from your WHOOP account settings, which revokes the app's access.
